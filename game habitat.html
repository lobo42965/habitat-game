🧭 Habitat Explorer!

Hola, explorers! First, give your explorer a name. Then walk to every station and find where plants live!

What is your explorer’s name?

How to play (groups of 3): Walk with the arrow keys or WASD (or the joystick on a touchscreen). Press Space or tap Jump to jump! Use the map on the left to find the stations. Walk into a glowing ring, then press E or tap Explore. There are 3 stations: Land, Water, and Air. Look at the plants, read the questions at the bottom, and write your answers on paper!

Simple mode: this device cannot show the 3D world, so look at the plant pictures and the questions.
